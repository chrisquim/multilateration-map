# Multilateration Map

> An intuitive visual tool to map, inspect, and analyze overlapping signal ranges and geographic proximity circles.

[**Open the Web App**](https://chrisquim.github.io/multilateration-map/)

---

## What You Can Do

- 📡 **Find Overlap Areas**: Enter a location (as a geohash) and a radius in meters to see coverage zones and find where signals or distances overlap.
- 🎯 **Click Anywhere to Add**: Tap or click directly on the map to drop a point right where you want it.
- 🖐️ **Drag and Adjust on the Fly**: Grab any pin on the map and slide it around. The circle moves with you, and its location coordinates update in real time.
- 👁️ **Live Typing Preview**: Watch a preview circle appear and resize instantly on the map while you type your radius or geohash.
- ✏️ **Edit Right in the List**: Update any point's geohash or distance straight from the sidebar without opening extra menus.
- 🗺️ **Switch Views**: Choose between Dark Mode, Light Mode, standard street maps, or satellite view depending on what is easiest to see.
- ⚡ **Works Offline & Remembers Your Work**:
  - Map areas you visit are saved automatically on your device, making repeat views instant and usable offline.
  - Your added points and selected map view stay saved even when you close or refresh the page.
